# VibeKit

**Every app gets its own persistent AI coding agent — it builds, hosts, and keeps improving the app over time (database + live domain included), driven from your phone or CLI.**

[**📱 Get the iOS app**](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vibekit-agent-devops/id6760206636) · [**vibekit.bot**](https://vibekit.bot) · [**@VibeKitBot**](https://twitter.com/VibeKitBot)

</div>

---

Describe what you want and an agent builds it, ships it to a live URL, and stays with the app to keep improving it — long after your laptop is closed. Bring your own Claude or OpenAI key at zero markup, or use pay-as-you-go credits (with a free tier to start).

- **Build from your phone.** Native iOS app + web dashboard + CLI.
- **A persistent agent per app.** Not a one-shot generator — it keeps maintaining and evolving the app.
- **Hosted + live by default.** Runs 24/7 on managed containers at a live domain, optional database add-on.
- **Bring your own key.** Claude or OpenAI at wholesale rates, or credits.

| Repo | What it is |

| [**vibekit-agent**](https://github.com/VibeKit-Bot/vibekit-agent) | Control your local machine / Claude Code from Telegram or the VibeKit app ([npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/vibekit-agent)) |
