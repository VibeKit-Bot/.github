# VibeKit

**Every app gets its own persistent AI coding agent. It builds, hosts and keeps improving the app over time (database and live domain included), driven from your phone or the CLI.**

[**📱 Get the iOS app**](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vibekit-agent-devops/id6760206636) · [**vibekit.bot**](https://vibekit.bot) · [**@VibeKitBot**](https://twitter.com/VibeKitBot)

</div>

---

Describe what you want and an agent builds it, ships it to a live URL, and stays with the app to keep improving it, long after your laptop is closed. Bring your own Claude or OpenAI key at zero markup, or use pay-as-you-go credits (with free credits to start).

- **Build from your phone.** Native iOS app, web dashboard and CLI.
- **A persistent agent per app.** Not a one-shot generator: it keeps maintaining and evolving the app.
- **Hosted and live by default.** Runs 24/7 on managed containers at a live domain, with an optional database add-on.
- **Bring your own key.** Claude or OpenAI at wholesale rates, or credits.

## What's in this org

| Repo | What it is |
|------|------------|
| [**kahneman-loop**](https://github.com/VibeKit-Bot/kahneman-loop) | The Kahneman Loop: stops AI agents from saying "done" when it isn't (the deploy hadn't finished, the test had failed). On our production traffic: 40% fewer false claims, for about 1% more tokens and 1% more time. One file, plus a drop-in Claude Code hook |
| [**templates**](https://github.com/VibeKit-Bot/templates) | The starter templates your first app can begin from |
| [**vibekit-mcp**](https://github.com/VibeKit-Bot/vibekit-mcp) | MCP server: drive VibeKit from Claude Desktop, Cursor, or any MCP client ([npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/vibekit-mcp)) |
| [**vibekit-cli**](https://github.com/VibeKit-Bot/vibekit-cli) | CLI: build, deploy, and manage your apps from the terminal ([npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/vibekit-cli)) |
| [**vibekit-agent**](https://github.com/VibeKit-Bot/vibekit-agent) | Control your local machine or Claude Code from Telegram or the VibeKit app ([npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/vibekit-agent)) |
